About PNG Events Hub

One place to find what is happening across Papua New Guinea, from cultural festivals and sports days to workshops, markets and university events.

Our mission

Community events are often shared only by word of mouth or on scattered social media pages, so many people miss them. PNG Events Hub brings listings together in one simple, easy-to-read website that works on a phone as well as a computer.

Who this site is for

Residents and families

Find free or low-cost activities nearby and see dates and locations at a glance.

Students

Discover campus events, workshops and career activities across universities.

Event organisers

Reach a wider audience and let people register or get in touch easily.
